
LeetCode 121 – Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock

Topic: Arrays | Greedy

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Given an array prices where prices[i] represents the stock price on the ith day, find the maximum profit by buying on one day and selling on a future day.

If no profit is possible, return 0.

2. APPROACH – GREEDY (ONE PASS)

- Initialize minPrice = Integer.MAX_VALUE to track the minimum buying price.
- Initialize maxProfit = 0 to track maximum profit.
- Traverse the array using an enhanced for loop.
- If the current price is smaller than minPrice, update minPrice.
- Calculate profit = price - minPrice.
- If the calculated profit is greater than maxProfit, update maxProfit.
- Continue until all prices are checked.
- Finally, return maxProfit.

3. DRY RUN

Input: [7, 1, 5, 3, 6, 4]

Price | minPrice | Profit | maxProfit
--------------------------------------
  7   |    7     |   0    |     0
  1   |    1     |   0    |     0
  5   |    1     |   4    |     4
  3   |    1     |   2    |     4
  6   |    1     |   5    |     5
  4   |    1     |   3    |     5

Output: 5

Buy at price = 1
Sell at price = 6

Maximum Profit = 6 - 1 = 5

4. COMPLEXITY ANALYSIS

Time Complexity: O(n)
Traverses the array once.

Space Complexity: O(1)
Uses only two variables without extra space.

5. KEY INTERVIEW POINTS

1. Why initialize minPrice = Integer.MAX_VALUE?
To ensure the first price automatically becomes the minimum.

2. Why initialize maxProfit = 0?
Because if no profitable transaction is possible, the answer should be 0.

3. Why use Greedy Approach?
We maintain the lowest buying price seen so far and calculate the best selling profit.

4. Why update minPrice before calculating profit?
To maintain the minimum price available up to the current day.

5. Can we buy and sell on the same day?
The algorithm allows zero profit, but the selling day must not precede the buying day.

6. Why is the solution O(n)?
Because we traverse the array only once.

CORE LOGIC

Track the minimum price seen so far. For every current price, calculate the possible profit and update maxProfit if it is higher.
